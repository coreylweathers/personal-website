## A family learning product

What began as a fractions experiment is becoming a broader way for families to learn together. The interesting questions are not only technical: they are about confidence, explanation, attention, and what a parent sees that software usually misses.

## Conversational AI experiments

Small prototypes exploring voice, repair, latency, transparency, and the difference between a system that can respond and one a person can understand.

## An independent publishing system

This site is part of the project: a durable home for essays, build logs, transcripts, and public thinking without handing the archive to an algorithm.

The stack matters when it helps an idea survive contact with reality. Detailed tools and architecture belong in the build logs, where they can serve the work instead of becoming the identity.
